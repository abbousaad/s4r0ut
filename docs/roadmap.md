# Roadmap

## Goal

Make `s4r0ut` the best Moroccan password-pattern dictionary for defensive
security research.

## Quality Principles

- Moroccan signal over generic volume
- Reproducible generation over opaque dumps
- Defensive framing over offensive hype
- Transparent seed categories
- No private leaks or personal data

## Release Profiles

- `mini`: compact, synthetic, quick for demos and awareness
- `standard`: larger Moroccan-focused list for authorized internal testing
- `extended`: broad generated variants for research
- `strict`: only entries with clear Moroccan origin

## Next Research Areas

- Regional Darija vocabulary
- Amazigh terms and transliterations
- Hassaniya terms and transliterations
- Moroccan family names
- Club chants and culturally common phrases
- Mobile-number-inspired patterns without real numbers
- Keyboard layouts and AZERTY/QWERTY variants used in Morocco
